Key Findings

The top three products generated 50.5% of total revenue, with all three belonging to the Dwellings of Eldervale product line.

After removing out-of-period checkout activity and aligning the dates, the real cart abandonment rate was only 7.0%, with 7 of 100 legitimate carts abandoned.

Paid Social generated 90,978 pageviews and Organic Search generated 80,482, together accounting for approximately 95.4% of categorized traffic.

Recommendations

  1. Prioritize holiday reorders for the top-selling products, especially the Dwellings of Eldervale line; the top three products alone generated 50.5% of total revenue.
  2. Pause DA | Sales until conversion tracking is verified and redirect its $12,917.08 toward controlled retests of historically efficient campaigns such as DA | Sales | ATC.
  3. Focus Q4 resources on generating qualified traffic and promoting strong products rather than making checkout abandonment the primary issue, since the date-aligned analysis found only 7 abandoned carts out of 100 legitimate carts.
